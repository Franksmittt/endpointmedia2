**Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)**

This Annex forms part of the Master Services Agreement between **Endpoint Media** (“Operator” for Client personal information processed to deliver the services) and **{{legal_entity_name}}** (“Client” / Responsible Party for Client-controlled personal information).

---

## 1. Roles

1.1 For personal information the Client controls and instructs Endpoint Media to process (e.g. lead form data, customer lists for ads audiences, analytics identifiers tied to Client properties), the Client is typically the **Responsible Party** and Endpoint Media is the **Operator**.

1.2 Endpoint Media is Responsible Party for its own business records (contracts, invoices, communications with the Client’s representatives).

---

## 2. Subject matter and duration

Processing continues for the term of the Agreement and for a reasonable retention period thereafter for legal, tax, and dispute purposes, unless deletion is required sooner by law or written instruction (subject to retention duties).

---

## 3. Nature and purpose of processing

Purposes may include:

- Delivering Google Ads / Meta / SEO / website services under the SoW  
- Conversion tracking, analytics configuration, and reporting  
- Communicating with Client contacts about the engagement  
- Invoicing and contract administration  

---

## 4. Types of personal information

Depending on the SoW, may include: names, email addresses, phone numbers, online identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from ads/analytics, form submission contents, and business contact details of Client personnel.

**Special personal information** will not be intentionally processed unless the Client expressly instructs it in writing and confirms a lawful basis.

---

## 5. Operator obligations

Endpoint Media will:

- Process personal information only on documented Client instructions (including this Agreement/SoW) unless required by law  
- Apply reasonable technical and organisational security measures appropriate to the risk  
- Ensure personnel with access are bound by confidentiality  
- Not engage sub-operators for Client personal information without reasonable arrangements imposing similar protections (e.g. hosting, email, analytics, ads platforms). Platforms such as Google and Meta are engaged as part of the service with Client knowledge  
- Assist the Client, where reasonably practicable and at Client cost if material, with data subject requests and breach notifications relating to Operator processing  
- Notify the Client without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal information security compromise affecting Client personal information processed as Operator  
- On termination, return or delete Client personal information on request, except where retention is required by law or for establishing legal claims  

---

## 6. Client obligations

The Client warrants that:

- It has a **lawful basis** to collect and share personal information with Endpoint Media and advertising platforms  
- Its privacy notices and consent mechanisms (where required) are adequate for the intended ads/analytics uses  
- Instructions to Endpoint Media will not knowingly require unlawful processing  

---

## 7. Cross-border transfers

Ads, analytics, email, and hosting providers may process data in other countries. The Client authorises such transfers as necessary to perform the SoW, subject to POPIA transfer conditions. Endpoint Media will use reputable providers and contractual protections where available.

---

## 8. Security incidents

“Security compromise” has the meaning contemplated under POPIA. Endpoint Media will investigate, contain, and cooperate with the Client’s notification duties where Operator processing is involved.

---

## 9. Precedence

If this Annex conflicts with a platform’s mandatory terms (Google / Meta), the parties will cooperate to stay compliant; platform terms may govern account use. As between the parties, the MSA precedence clause applies.
