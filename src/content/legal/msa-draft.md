Endpoint Media has **no obligation** to commence work, grant dashboard access, or provide deliverables until **both** of the following have occurred:

1. This Agreement (including Key Commercial Terms and applicable SoW(s)) has been **digitally signed / accepted** by the Client; and  
2. The Client has **paid the first invoice** in full.

Until then, any preliminary discussion is non-binding and may be withdrawn.

## 4. Term and renewal

4.1 **Initial Term:** Three (3) months from the Activation Date (the date both signature and first payment are complete). The Initial Term is intended for onboarding, learning, and optimisation.

4.2 **Renewal Terms:** Thereafter, this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of six (6) months each (“Renewal Term”), unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the then-current term.

4.3 Fees for Renewal Terms are as set out in the Key Commercial Terms or a written fee update issued before renewal.

---

## 5. Fees, invoicing, and non-VAT status

5.1 Fees are **flat amounts in South African Rand (ZAR)** as stated in the SoW / Key Commercial Terms.

5.2 Endpoint Media is **not VAT-registered**. Amounts due are stated without VAT. Payment documents will use wording substantially as follows:  
   **“Amount Due (VAT not applicable – vendor not registered).”**

5.3 **Ad spend** (Google Ads, Meta Ads, or similar) is paid by the Client **directly** to the advertising platform. Ad spend is not Endpoint Media’s fee and is not invoiced by Endpoint Media as media cost.

5.4 Invoices are payable within the period stated on the invoice (default: seven (7) days unless otherwise agreed in writing).

5.5 Endpoint Media may **suspend** services for overdue amounts after written notice. Suspension does not waive amounts already due.

---

## 6. Early cancellation (CPA-aware model)

6.1 The Client may cancel early by written notice. Cancellation is **not** modelled solely as “100% of all remaining fees for the term.”

6.2 On early cancellation by the Client during the Initial Term or a Renewal Term, the Client shall pay:

   **(a) Clawback of waived setup / strategy fee** — If Endpoint Media waived or discounted an onboarding, setup, or strategy fee on condition of completing the agreed term, the Client shall pay the waived/discounted amount as stated in the Key Commercial Terms; **and**

   **(b) Notice-period fees** — Fees that would have accrued for the applicable notice period (default: thirty (30) days of retainer fees, or such other notice-period fee stated in the Key Commercial Terms).

6.3 Amounts already invoiced and unpaid remain due. Work product not paid for need not be delivered or licensed.

6.4 **CPA dual track:** Where the Client’s annual turnover or asset value is below R2 million (or is unknown / undeclared), Endpoint Media will treat the Client as potentially CPA-protected and will not rely on a blunt “pay all remaining term fees” clause as the sole cancellation model. Where the Client declares turnover/assets **above** R2 million, the parties may agree additional commercial terms in writing, still subject to applicable law.
