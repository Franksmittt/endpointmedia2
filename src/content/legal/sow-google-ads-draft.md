## 1. Service summary

Endpoint Media will manage Google Ads campaigns for the Client on a monthly retainer basis, including account structure, keyword/search theme strategy, bidding recommendations within agreed budgets, ad copy iteration, conversion tracking review (within access granted), and periodic performance reporting.

**Meta Ads** may be included only if selected in onboarding / Key Commercial Terms; otherwise this SoW is Google Ads only.

---

## 2. What is included

- Campaign build / restructure aligned to agreed goals  
- Ongoing optimisation during the term (bids, queries, creatives, audiences as applicable)  
- Conversion tracking recommendations (GA4 / GTM access required)  
- Monthly written or call-based performance summary  
- Reasonable async communication during South African business hours  

## 3. What is excluded (unless separately quoted)

- Website development or CRO implementation beyond recommendations  
- Creative production (video/photography design) beyond ad text/extensions  
- Guaranteed rankings, CPA, ROAS, or lead volumes  
- Payment of media / ad spend  
- Legal compliance review of regulated offers (Client remains responsible)

---

## 4. Ad spend

Ad spend is paid by the Client **directly** to Google (and Meta if applicable). Endpoint Media does not mark up or invoice media spend as a “Tax Invoice” media line. Endpoint Media fees are separate flat ZAR retainers.
