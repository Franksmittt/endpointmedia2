Quality Score itself is a highly sensitive diagnostic tool, rated on a scale of one to ten, which Google utilizes to measure the overall relevance, safety, and user experience of a specific advertisement and its corresponding destination landing page.14 The Quality Score algorithm is heavily weighted upon three core pillars: expected click-through rate (CTR), ad relevance (how closely the ad copy matches the user's search query intent), and the landing page experience.14 Most traditional digital marketing agencies operating in Randburg and Sandton focus their optimization efforts exclusively on the first two pillars—endlessly adjusting ad copy and refining keyword targeting parameters. They categorically neglect the third and arguably most critical pillar, the landing page experience, because optimizing it requires advanced, full-stack web development capabilities rather than simple media buying skills.
The financial penalty extracted by Google for a poor landing page experience is exceedingly severe. Empirical data surrounding the Google Ads auction indicates that a poor landing page experience can inflate Cost Per Click by up to four hundred percent and severely restrict available impression share to below thirty percent of the total available market.16 Conversely, achieving an "Excellent" Quality Score of eight or nine out of ten triggers an automatic cost reduction discount of approximately thirty-five percent compared to the baseline auction price.16
