PRICING PRINCIPLES
================================================================================
• Web builds: Once-off project fees. You own the code. Post-launch maintenance is mapped to build complexity.
• Google Ads: Flat-fee management by ad-spend tier. Never a percentage of ad spend.
• Meta Ads: Scoped per engagement; ad spend paid directly to Meta.
• Ad spend (Google/Meta): Billed separately to the platforms — not marked up by Endpoint.
• After year one: Hosting/domain renewal typically R200–R500/month (traffic-dependent).
• Optional maintenance: From R500/month (see Maintenance section).
• Free architecture audit available via /contact (recommended before choosing a tier).
